About Me

I'm Vilshan Rangana, a UX/UI Designer and Web Developer currently pursuing my studies at the University of Vavuniya.

I enjoy exploring the intersection between design and technology — creating interfaces that not only look good but are also simple, accessible, and enjoyable to use.

My journey started with an interest in visual design and gradually grew into a passion for web development and user experience. Today, I work across the design and development process, from understanding a problem and creating wireframes to designing interfaces and bringing them to life with code.

I'm continuously learning, experimenting with new technologies, and building projects that challenge me to become a better designer and developer.
